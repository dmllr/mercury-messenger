
### English (en), default

**App name:**
Mercury - Private Messenger

**Short description:**
Encrypted peer-to-peer messaging, voice, and video calls with no central servers.

**Full description:**
Mercury is an open-source peer-to-peer (P2P) messenger built for private communication. Instead of routing traffic through central servers, Mercury connects you directly to your contacts. Your text messages, voice and video calls, images, and files are end-to-end encrypted and transferred straight between devices.

Features:
• Direct P2P messaging and file transfer (no intermediary servers)
• End-to-end encrypted voice and video calls (WebRTC)
• Anonymous identity via unique ID (no phone number or email required)
• Account recovery via 12-word recovery phrase
• Encrypted, password-protected local backups
• Cross-platform C++ core

Source code:
https://github.com/dmllr/mercury-messenger
